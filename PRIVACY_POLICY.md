# Dark Chat Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** November 2, 2026  
**Application:** Dark Chat for Android  
**Android package:** `com.darkchat.app`  
**Developer / publisher:** Dark Chat

Dark Chat ("the App," "we," "our," or "us") is a messaging and communication application. This Privacy Policy explains the information processed when you use Dark Chat, how it is used and shared, and the choices available to you.

## 1. Information we collect and process

**Account and profile information.** When you register or use Dark Chat, we process your chosen username, password authentication information, account identifier, display name, profile image (if provided), and profile details such as your status or bio. The App generates an internal email-style identifier from your username for authentication; it does not require you to enter a personal email address in its current sign-up form. Authentication credentials are handled through our authentication service.

**Messages and user content.** We process the text messages, voice messages, images, videos, files, group information, and other content you choose to send or upload. Messages include information such as the sender, conversation, content type, and timestamps. Content is stored and delivered so that conversations and sharing features can work.

**Calls and call history.** Voice and video calls may process microphone audio, camera video, signaling data, identifiers for call participants, and basic call records such as call type, time, and connection status. Calls use real-time communication technology; the App does not provide a feature to record the contents of calls. Other participants may have their own ability to record or capture what they receive.

**Notifications and device information.** To send notifications, the Android App uses Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) tokens. We store an associated user identifier, push token, device platform, app package name, and token registration/update timestamps. Our service providers may also process technical information, such as IP addresses, device details, and diagnostic information needed to operate their services.

**Permissions.** Depending on which features you use, Dark Chat may request access to your microphone (voice messages and calls), camera (video calls), files or selected media (attachments), and Android notifications. These permissions can be controlled through your device settings. If you deny a permission, the corresponding feature may not work.

## Marketplace, Store and Order Information (Dark Chat v1.5)

Dark Chat includes an optional paid storefront activation feature. If you request a storefront, we collect your account ID, selected payment method, payment transaction reference, request status, and review dates. For JazzCash, payments are made outside the App and **a platform administrator manually checks payment before granting store access**. A transaction reference alone does not prove a payment occurred. Google Pay is not an automatically verified checkout method unless a genuine merchant payment integration is separately configured and confirmed.

Store owners may publish business names, greetings, product descriptions, categories, prices and promotional product photographs. This information is visible to other signed-in users. The C Anyway feature can automatically respond to incoming private chat messages using a store owner's configured greeting and keyword responses.

When you order a product, we collect the selected product, quantity, your name, phone number, delivery address, optional instructions, order status and time. Your order details become visible to the relevant store owner and platform administrators, so they can handle the order. Store owners are independently responsible for how they use customer information in fulfilling orders. Only provide contact and delivery information when necessary.

Dark Chat may display labeled sponsored banners in its announcements area. These may link to an external third-party website. If third-party advertising networks are activated in a future update, their SDKs and advertising data practices will be disclosed here before deployment. Storing an advertising publisher ID does not itself activate a network SDK.

## 2. Why we use information

We use the information described above to:
- Create and authenticate accounts and maintain profiles.
- Deliver private and group conversations, messages, attachments, and call signaling.
- Enable voice/video calls and show call history.
- Send notifications about messages and incoming calls.
- Operate, maintain, troubleshoot, and protect the App and its infrastructure.
- Respond to privacy requests and comply with applicable legal obligations.

We do not use the current App to serve third-party advertising or operate a data-selling service.

## 3. Sharing and service providers

Information may be processed by the infrastructure providers that power the App, including:
- **Supabase:** authentication, databases, real-time features, file storage, and server-side functions.
- **Google Firebase / Firebase Cloud Messaging:** push notification delivery and associated device tokens.
- **Other networking and delivery providers:** services needed for real-time connectivity and to load app resources.

Information you voluntarily send in a conversation is shared with its recipients. In group chats, your messages and shared content can be seen by members who have access to that group.

We may disclose information if required by applicable law, to respond to lawful requests, or to protect users and the service where legally permitted.

## 4. Important notice about uploaded media

**Media attachments currently use publicly accessible storage URLs.** This means anyone who obtains a direct link to an uploaded image, audio recording, video, or file may be able to access that file, even if they are not a participant in the original conversation. **Please do not use Dark Chat to share highly sensitive or confidential files.** We do not claim that Dark Chat currently provides end-to-end encryption.

Deleting a message in the App may replace its chat content with a deletion notice, but it does not necessarily immediately remove previously uploaded file objects, links, copies, or content saved by recipients.

## 5. Retention and deletion

We keep account, conversation, file, notification-token, and call-related data for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the App, maintain records, resolve disputes, comply with legal obligations, or protect the service.

The App offers options to remove messages from your own view and to request **Delete for Everyone** for messages you sent. A message deleted for everyone may remain represented by a notice such as “This message was deleted.” Deleting messages does not guarantee deletion of recipient copies or previously shared media URLs.

The current App does not include a complete in-app account-deletion workflow. To request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information or account, use the privacy contact method below. We will assess and respond to reasonable requests subject to applicable law and technical limitations.

## 6. Security

We use authentication, access controls, and service-provider infrastructure intended to protect information. However, no transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. In particular, please review the notice about publicly accessible media links in Section 4.

## 7. International processing

Our technology providers may process or store information in locations outside your country. Their processing is subject to their applicable service terms and privacy practices.

## 8. Children's privacy

Dark Chat is not intended for children under 13. If we become aware that we have collected personal information from a child under 13 without appropriate authorization, we will take reasonable steps to review and address it. Parents or guardians may contact us using the method below.

## 9. Your choices

You can choose what you share, update available profile information, disable notifications, and control camera and microphone permissions through Android settings. You may stop using the App at any time. To make a privacy request that is not supported in the App, contact us as described below.

## 10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when features, providers, or legal obligations change. An updated policy will be published at this URL with a revised effective date. Please review it periodically.
